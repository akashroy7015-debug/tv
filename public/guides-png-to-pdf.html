How to Convert PNG to PDF

Turn images and scans into a clean, shareable PDF — privately.

PDF is the universal "send it to anyone" document format. If you have a screenshot, a scanned form, a receipt, or a photo of a signed page, turning it into a PDF makes it look professional, prints predictably, and opens identically on every device. This guide shows how to convert PNG (or JPG) images into PDF without uploading anything.

Why turn an image into a PDF?

  • It looks official: forms, invoices and applications are expected as PDFs, not raw images.
  • Consistent printing: a PDF fixes the page size, so it prints the same everywhere.
  • Easier to send: one PDF is tidier than a folder of loose image files.
  • Harder to accidentally edit: recipients see exactly what you sent.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and select the Image tab.
  • Drop in your .png or .jpg file.
  • Set Convert to → PDF.
  • Click Convert and download your PDF.

Tips for the best-looking PDF

Start from the highest-resolution image you have — the PDF can only be as sharp as its source. For scanned documents, make sure the scan is straight and well-lit before converting. If you are converting a photo of a paper page, crop out the background first so the document fills the page. For text-heavy scans you want to keep crisp, a PNG source preserves sharp edges better than a heavily-compressed JPG.

Is it private?

Yes. The image-to-PDF conversion happens in your browser — ideal for receipts, contracts, IDs and medical forms that should never be uploaded to a random website. Nothing is sent to a server or stored.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert a JPG to PDF the same way? Yes — the steps are identical; just drop a JPG instead of a PNG.

Will the PDF be high quality? The PDF preserves your image at its original resolution, so quality matches the file you started with.

Can I make a multi-page PDF? Convert each page and combine them, or use a PDF merge step — each image becomes a clean, full-page sheet.
