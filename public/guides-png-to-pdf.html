How to Convert PNG to PDF

Free, fast, and private — right in your browser.

PNG is a lossless image format with transparency support. PDF is a portable document format that looks identical everywhere. Converting PNG to PDF gives you a file that's easier to use, share, or open where PNG isn't supported.

Why convert PNG to PDF?

  • Looks the same on every device
  • One tidy file for printing or emailing
  • Accepted by forms and document systems

Steps

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop your .png file (or click browse).
  • Set the output format to PDF.
  • Click Convert and download your PDF file.

Is it private?

Image conversions run entirely in your browser — your file is never uploaded to a server.
