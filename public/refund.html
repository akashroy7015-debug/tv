Refund Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This policy explains how billing, cancellation, and refunds work at FileMorph. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record, who handles billing, invoices, and applicable taxes on our behalf.

1. Payments are non-refundable

All payments — subscriptions (Pro, Team) and pay-as-you-go credits — are non-refundable once paid. Because FileMorph delivers digital access immediately upon payment, we do not offer refunds for completed charges, including for partially used billing periods or unused credits. Please review your plan and the free tier before purchasing.

2. Cancel anytime

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account or by emailing us. When you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not refund the current or past periods.

3. Pay-as-you-go credits

Credits are delivered to your account instantly and never expire, so you can use them whenever you like. Credit purchases are non-refundable.

4. Duplicate or accidental charges

If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or billed in genuine error, contact us and we will review and correct the incorrect charge.

5. Questions before you buy

Want to make sure FileMorph fits your needs first? Use the free plan to try the converter and tools, or email support@filemorph.shop with any questions before purchasing. We respond within 1–2 business days.

6. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the consumer-protection laws of your country. Where local law grants mandatory refund or cancellation rights (for example, EU/UK cooling-off periods), those rights apply.

7. Contact

Questions about a charge or cancellation? Email support@filemorph.shop or use our Contact page. See also our Terms.
