Refund Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

We want you to be happy with FileMorph. This policy explains when and how you can get a refund. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record, who handles billing, invoices, and applicable taxes on our behalf.

1. 7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with a paid subscription (Pro or Team), you may request a full refund within 7 days of your first payment. Just email us — no complicated forms. This applies to your initial purchase on a plan.

2. Recurring renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each billing period. You can cancel any time from your account or by emailing us, and your plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for. If a renewal charged you unexpectedly, contact us within 7 days of that charge and we will review it — we routinely refund accidental or unused renewals.

3. Pay-as-you-go credits

Pay-as-you-go credit purchases are non-refundable. Credits are delivered to your account instantly and never expire, so you can use them whenever you like. (This does not affect refunds for duplicate or accidental charges — see section 4.)

4. Duplicate or accidental charges

If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or billed in error, we will refund the incorrect charge in full. Just let us know.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@filemorph.shop from the address on your account, and include your order ID or the email used at checkout. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card provider.

6. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or cancellation rights you have under the consumer-protection laws of your country. Where local law grants stronger rights (for example, EU/UK cooling-off periods), those rights apply.

7. Contact

Questions about a charge or refund? Email support@filemorph.shop or use our Contact page. See also our Terms.
