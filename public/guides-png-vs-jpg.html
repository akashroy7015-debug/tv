PNG vs JPG

Two classics — and when each one wins.

JPG is lossy and great for photos; PNG is lossless and great for graphics and transparency.

Use JPG when…

  • It's a photograph or complex image with gradients.
  • You want the smallest file for the web.
  • You don't need a transparent background.

Use PNG when…

  • You need transparency (logos, icons, overlays).
  • The image has sharp edges or text (screenshots).
  • You want lossless quality for editing.

Quick comparison

  • Compression: JPG lossy, PNG lossless.
  • Transparency: PNG yes, JPG no.
  • Best for: JPG photos, PNG graphics.

Switch easily: PNG to JPG or JPG to PNG. For the web, consider WebP.
