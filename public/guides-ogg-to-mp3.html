How to Convert OGG to MP3

Free, fast, and private — right in your browser.

OGG is an open, compressed audio format. MP3 is the universal compressed audio format. Converting OGG to MP3 gives you a file that's easier to use, share, or open where OGG isn't supported.

Why convert OGG to MP3?

  • Plays on every device and music app
  • Small files that are easy to share
  • The safe default for audio

Steps

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Audio tab.
  • Drop your .ogg file (or click browse).
  • Set the output format to MP3.
  • Click Convert and download your MP3 file.

Is it private?

Audio converts right in your browser on your own device — nothing is uploaded. The first conversion loads the engine once, then it's quick.

Convert OGG to MP3 now: open the FileMorph converter, pick the Audio tab, drop your file, and click Convert.
