How to Convert PDF to JPG

Turn PDF pages into images you can share, post or embed.

Sometimes you don't want a whole document — you want a picture of the page. Converting a PDF to JPG is perfect for posting a page to social media, adding it to a slide, creating a thumbnail, or sending a quick preview to someone who won't open a PDF. Here's how to do it privately in your browser.

Why convert PDF to JPG?

  • Easy sharing: images display inline in chats and social posts; PDFs don't.
  • Embedding: drop a page into a presentation, document or web page as an image.
  • Previews & thumbnails: show what a document looks like at a glance.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Document tab.
  • Drop in your .pdf file.
  • Set Convert to → JPG (choose PNG instead if you need transparency or sharper text).
  • Click Convert and download. A multi-page PDF gives you each page as an image (delivered together).

JPG or PNG for pages?

Choose JPG for the smallest file — ideal for photo-heavy pages and quick sharing. Choose PNG when the page is mostly text or line art and you want the crispest possible result, since PNG is lossless and keeps text edges sharp. For most quick uses, JPG is perfectly fine.

Is it private?

Yes. PDF-to-image conversion runs entirely in your browser — your document is never uploaded to a server. That makes it safe for statements, contracts and other sensitive pages.

Frequently asked questions

What about multi-page PDFs? Each page becomes its own image, delivered together so you get them all.

Will the image be sharp? Pages are rendered at a high resolution for clear text and detail; use PNG for the sharpest text.

Is anything uploaded? No — it all happens locally on your device.
