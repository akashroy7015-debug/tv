What Is WebP?

Google's image format built for a faster web.

WebP is an image format developed by Google that delivers smaller files than JPG and PNG at similar quality. It supports both lossy and lossless compression and transparency — combining the strengths of JPG and PNG in one format.

Why it matters

WebP images are often 25–35% smaller, so pages load faster and use less data. Every modern browser supports it, which is why so many websites now serve WebP.

When to convert WebP

Some older apps and tools still expect PNG or JPG. If you download a WebP and your editor won't open it, convert it — see WebP to PNG or WebP to JPG.

WebP vs PNG vs JPG

  • Photos: WebP (lossy) beats JPG on size.
  • Graphics/transparency: WebP (lossless) beats PNG on size.
  • Maximum compatibility: fall back to JPG/PNG.
