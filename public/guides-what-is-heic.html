What Is HEIC?

The format behind your iPhone photos.

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the image format Apple devices use by default since iOS 11. It stores photos using the modern HEIF standard, which compresses images far more efficiently than JPG.

Why Apple uses it

HEIC files are typically about half the size of an equivalent JPG at the same quality. That means your iPhone stores roughly twice as many photos, and they keep richer detail and support features like depth maps and live photos.

The downside

Compatibility. Many Windows programs, older devices, websites and apps can't open HEIC. That's why people convert HEIC to JPG or PNG before sharing or uploading.

How to open or convert HEIC

  • On a Mac or iPhone, HEIC opens natively.
  • On Windows or the web, convert it first — see our HEIC to JPG guide.

If you mostly share photos, you can also set your iPhone to capture in "Most Compatible" (JPG) under Settings → Camera → Formats.
