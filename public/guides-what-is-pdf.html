What Is a PDF?

The format for documents that need to look exactly right.

PDF (Portable Document Format) was created by Adobe to share documents that look identical on any device or printer. Fonts, images, spacing and page breaks are all locked in.

Why PDFs are everywhere

  • They open with free readers on every platform.
  • The layout never shifts, so forms and contracts stay intact.
  • They print exactly as designed.

What PDFs aren't great for

Editing. A PDF is a finished document, not a working draft — that's what DOCX is for. See PDF vs DOCX.
