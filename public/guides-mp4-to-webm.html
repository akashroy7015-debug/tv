How to Convert MP4 to WebM

Free, fast, and private — right in your browser.

MP4 is the most widely supported video format. WebM is an open, web-optimized video format. Converting MP4 to WebM gives you a file that's easier to use, share, or open where MP4 isn't supported.

Why convert MP4 to WebM?

  • Smaller, web-optimized files
  • Open format, no licensing
  • Streams smoothly online

Steps

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Video tab.
  • Drop your .mp4 file (or click browse).
  • Set the output format to WebM.
  • Click Convert and download your WebM file.

Is it private?

Video converts right in your browser on your own device — nothing is uploaded. The first conversion loads the engine once; larger videos take longer.
