What Is FLAC?

CD-quality sound without the giant files.

FLAC (Free Lossless Audio Codec) compresses audio with zero quality loss. It's typically 40–60% smaller than WAV while keeping every detail of the original.

Why people use FLAC

  • Perfect, lossless quality for archiving music.
  • Smaller than WAV, so it saves space.
  • Open and royalty-free.

FLAC vs MP3 vs WAV

  • FLAC: lossless, medium size — best for keeping masters.
  • WAV: lossless, largest — best for editing.
  • MP3: lossy, smallest — best for casual listening.

Compatibility

Not every device plays FLAC. For phones and car stereos, many people convert to MP3 — see FLAC to MP3.
