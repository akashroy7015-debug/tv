How to Convert M4A to MP3

Make iPhone voice memos and Apple audio play everywhere.

M4A is the format your iPhone uses for voice memos and that Apple uses for music downloads. It's efficient and good quality — but some older players, car stereos, and apps still expect MP3. Converting M4A to MP3 makes your audio play on virtually any device.

M4A vs MP3

M4A usually contains AAC audio, which is technically a bit more efficient than MP3 at the same size. But MP3 is the most universally supported audio format in existence. For most listening — voice notes, music, podcasts — a good-quality MP3 sounds the same to the ear and works everywhere.

Why convert M4A to MP3?

  • Universal playback: old players, car systems and basic apps all support MP3.
  • Sharing: MP3 is the safe choice when sending audio to anyone.
  • Upload compatibility: many platforms expect MP3.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Audio tab.
  • Drop in your .m4a file.
  • Set Convert to → MP3.
  • Click Convert and download your MP3.

A note on quality

Both M4A (AAC) and MP3 are lossy, so converting between them re-compresses the audio slightly. For voice memos and everyday music the difference is inaudible. If you plan to edit the audio repeatedly, keep your original M4A as the master and export MP3 only for sharing. See our lossless vs lossy guide for why.

Frequently asked questions

Will my voice memo lose quality? A tiny amount from re-compression, but it stays clear for speech.

Is MP3 worse than M4A? Marginally less efficient, but far more compatible — usually the better choice for sharing.

Does it keep the length/metadata? The audio converts fully; basic playback is unaffected.
