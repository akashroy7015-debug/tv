GIF vs MP4

Why a tiny video often beats an "animated image".

GIFs feel made for the internet, but for short clips an MP4 video is usually smaller and sharper.

The problem with GIFs

GIF is an old format limited to 256 colors and no real compression for motion. A few seconds of GIF can be several megabytes and look grainy.

Why MP4 wins

MP4 uses modern video compression, so the same clip can be a fraction of the size with full color and smooth motion. Most platforms autoplay short MP4s on a loop, just like a GIF.

When a GIF still makes sense

  • Email signatures and old systems that only accept images.
  • Very short, simple animations (a few frames).

Need a real GIF anyway? Convert a clip with MP4 to GIF. Going the other way, MOV to MP4 keeps things compatible.
