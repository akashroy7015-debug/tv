How to Convert JPG to PDF

Turn photos and scans into a clean, professional PDF.

A JPG is fine for a snapshot, but when you need to submit a form, receipt, ID or signed page, a PDF looks far more professional and is what most organisations expect. Converting JPG to PDF wraps your image in a tidy, printable document — and with FileMorph it happens privately in your browser.

Why convert JPG to PDF?

  • Looks official: applications and forms expect PDFs, not loose photos.
  • Prints predictably: a PDF fixes the page size so it prints cleanly.
  • Tidy sharing: one PDF beats sending several separate image files.
  • Accepted everywhere: upload portals that reject images take PDFs.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop in your .jpg file.
  • Set Convert to → PDF.
  • Click Convert and download your PDF.

Tips for the best result

Use the highest-quality photo you have — the PDF is only as sharp as the image inside it. If you're photographing a paper document, shoot it in good light, hold the camera straight, and crop out the background first so the page fills the frame. For text documents, make sure the writing is in focus and readable before converting.

Is it private?

Yes — the conversion runs entirely in your browser, so your image never leaves your device. That's ideal for IDs, bank documents and other sensitive scans.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine several JPGs into one PDF? Convert each and merge them, or add pages one at a time — each image becomes a full page.

Will quality drop? No meaningful loss — your image is placed into the PDF at its original resolution.

Does it work for PNG too? Yes — see PNG to PDF.

Try it now: open the FileMorph converter, choose Image, drop your JPG, pick PDF, and click Convert.
