Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FileMorph ("we", "us", "our"), available at filemorph.shop, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services. By using FileMorph, you agree to this policy.

1. Files you convert

Image and PDF conversions run entirely in your browser using your device's own processing. Those files are not uploaded to our servers and we never see or store them. For other formats processed by our conversion service, files are handled only to perform the requested conversion and are not retained longer than necessary.

2. Account information

If you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. Authentication is provided by Supabase. We use this information to operate your account, verify your email, and provide access to paid features.

3. Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. When you purchase a plan, your payment details are entered on Lemon Squeezy's secure checkout — we never receive or store your card information. We receive only confirmation of your subscription status.

4. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in and to improve the service.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to FileMorph and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

5. Third-party services

We rely on the following providers, each with their own privacy policies: Cloudflare (hosting & security), Supabase (accounts), Lemon Squeezy (payments), and Google AdSense (advertising).

6. Data retention & your rights

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your associated personal information from our systems.

7. Children's privacy

FileMorph is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@filemorph.shop or visit our Contact page.
