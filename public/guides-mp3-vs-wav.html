MP3 vs WAV

Small and convenient, or lossless and large?

MP3 is a lossy, compressed format — small files that play everywhere. WAV is uncompressed and lossless — perfect quality but big files.

MP3: everyday audio

An MP3 is roughly a tenth the size of the same WAV. For listening on phones, podcasts and sharing, MP3 at 192–320 kbps sounds great and saves huge amounts of space.

WAV: studio quality

WAV keeps the exact original audio with no compression. It's the right choice for recording, editing and mastering, where you don't want to lose any detail. The cost is large files (about 10 MB per minute of stereo audio).

Which to choose

  • Listening / sharing: MP3.
  • Editing / archiving: WAV (or FLAC for lossless but smaller).
