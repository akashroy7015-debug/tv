How to Convert HEIC to PNG

Turn iPhone photos into lossless, universally-supported PNG images.

iPhones save photos as HEIC, which many apps and Windows tools can't open. Converting to PNG gives you a lossless image that opens everywhere and is ideal when you need maximum quality or plan to edit. (If you just want a smaller, share-friendly file, JPG is usually better — see HEIC to JPG.)

HEIC to PNG vs HEIC to JPG

Both make your iPhone photo openable everywhere. The difference: PNG is lossless (no compression artefacts, larger file) while JPG is lossy (much smaller, perfect for sharing). Choose PNG when you'll edit the image, need the crispest result, or want a transparent canvas to work on; choose JPG for everyday sharing and uploads.

Why convert HEIC to PNG?

  • Lossless quality: no compression artefacts — great for editing.
  • Universal support: PNG opens in every app, editor and OS.
  • Crisp detail: ideal for screenshots of photos, graphics work, or archiving.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop in your .heic file.
  • Set Convert to → PNG.
  • Click Convert and download your PNG.

Is it private?

Yes — HEIC decoding and conversion run in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded. Ideal for personal pictures.

Frequently asked questions

Is PNG better than JPG for HEIC? For quality/editing, yes; for small shareable files, JPG wins.

Why is the PNG large? PNG is lossless, so it stores more data than a compressed JPG.

Will it work for Live Photos? The still frame is converted; the short video portion is dropped.
