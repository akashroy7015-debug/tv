How to Convert MOV to MP4

Make QuickTime and iPhone videos play and upload anywhere.

MOV is the video format your iPhone and Mac record in, and it is excellent — but when you try to upload it to certain websites, send it to a Windows or Android user, or import it into some editors, it can stutter or get rejected. MP4 is the most widely supported video container on the planet, so converting MOV to MP4 is the simplest way to make a clip "just work" everywhere.

MOV vs MP4: what's the difference?

Both are "containers" that wrap video and audio streams. MOV is Apple's QuickTime container; MP4 is the cross-platform standard. The good news is that they often hold the same H.264/HEVC video inside, which means converting between them is usually fast and keeps quality essentially identical — you are mostly repackaging the streams rather than re-encoding everything from scratch.

Why convert MOV to MP4?

  • Universal playback: MP4 plays on Windows, Android, smart TVs and every browser.
  • Upload compatibility: many platforms and forms prefer or require MP4.
  • Smaller, tidier files: MP4 is well-optimised for streaming and sharing.
  • Editing: almost every video editor imports MP4 without fuss.

Keeping quality high

Because MOV and MP4 frequently share the same underlying codec, the converted MP4 should look practically identical to the original. Very long or 4K/60fps videos take more time and processing, so give the converter a moment — the result is worth it.

Frequently asked questions

Will converting MOV to MP4 reduce quality? In most cases the visual quality is preserved because the same video codec is reused inside the new container.

Why is my MOV so large? iPhone 4K and high-frame-rate footage is data-heavy; the MP4 is typically a similar size unless you re-encode at a lower bitrate.

Does the audio stay in sync? Yes — audio and video are converted together and remain aligned.
