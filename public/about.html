About FileMorph

Convert any file, online — without giving up your privacy.

FileMorph is an online file converter built around a simple idea: converting a file shouldn't mean handing it to a stranger's server. Most converters upload your photos and documents to the cloud. We took a different approach.

What we do

FileMorph converts images, documents, video, audio, and email formats from one place in your browser — no software to install. Image and PDF conversions happen entirely on your own device using modern browser technology, so those files never leave your computer.

Our promise: private by default, simple to use, and no bloated installs. Start with free conversions, and upgrade only when you need more.

Why we built it

We were tired of converter sites buried in ads, pop-ups, and upload limits — and uneasy about where our files were going. FileMorph is our answer: a fast, clean, privacy-respecting converter that does the job and gets out of your way.

How it works

  • Pick a category — image, document, video, audio, or email.
  • Drop your file and choose an output format.
  • Convert — images & PDFs process instantly in your browser.

Built on

FileMorph runs on Cloudflare for fast, secure global hosting, with accounts powered by Supabase and payments handled by Lemon Squeezy.
