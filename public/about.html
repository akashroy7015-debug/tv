About FileMorph

Convert any file, online — without giving up your privacy.

FileMorph is an online file converter built around a simple idea: converting a file shouldn't mean handing it to a stranger's server. Most converters upload your photos and documents to the cloud. We took a different approach.

What we do

FileMorph converts images, documents, video, audio, and email formats from one place in your browser — no software to install. Image and PDF conversions happen entirely on your own device using modern browser technology, so those files never leave your computer.

Our promise: private by default, simple to use, and no bloated installs. Start with free conversions, and upgrade only when you need more.

Why we built it

We were tired of converter sites buried in ads, pop-ups, and upload limits — and uneasy about where our files were going. FileMorph is our answer: a fast, clean, privacy-respecting converter that does the job and gets out of your way.

How it works

  • Pick a category — image, document, video, audio, or email.
  • Drop your file and choose an output format.
  • Convert — images & PDFs process instantly in your browser.

What you can convert

FileMorph handles the everyday conversions people actually need, across five categories:

  • Images: HEIC, WebP, PNG, JPG, TIFF, SVG, BMP, GIF and ICO.
  • Documents: PDF, DOCX, EPUB, PPTX, and conversions to HTML or plain text.
  • Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, plus video-to-GIF.
  • Audio: MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG and FLAC.
  • Email: EML, MSG and MBOX to PDF or HTML.

New to a format? Our guides explain the differences and walk through each conversion step by step.

What we value

  • Privacy first: image and PDF conversions run on your device and are never uploaded.
  • No clutter: a clean interface with no pop-ups or aggressive upsells.
  • Fair pricing: a genuine free tier, pay-as-you-go credits that never expire, and simple monthly plans.
  • Transparency: clear policies on data, payments and how each conversion works.

Built on

FileMorph runs on Cloudflare for fast, secure global hosting, with accounts powered by Supabase and payments handled by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. Heavier conversions are processed on our own secure conversion service and the files are not retained.
