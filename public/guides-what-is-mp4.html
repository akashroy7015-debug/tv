What Is MP4?

The video format that just works.

MP4 is the most widely supported video container. It typically holds H.264 video and AAC audio — a combination that plays on virtually every phone, browser, TV and editor.

Container vs codec

MP4 is the "box". Inside it are encoded video and audio streams (the codecs). That's why two MP4 files can behave differently — it depends on the codecs inside. See what is a codec.

Why convert to MP4

Formats like MOV, MKV or WebM don't always play everywhere. Converting them to MP4 (H.264/AAC) is the simplest fix for "this video won't open" problems.
