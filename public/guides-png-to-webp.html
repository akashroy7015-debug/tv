How to Convert PNG to WebP

Free, fast, and private — right in your browser.

PNG is a lossless image format with transparency support. WebP is a modern image format with excellent compression. Converting PNG to WebP gives you a file that's easier to use, share, or open where PNG isn't supported.

Why convert PNG to WebP?

  • Significantly smaller files at the same quality
  • Loads faster on websites
  • Supported by all modern browsers

Steps

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop your .png file (or click browse).
  • Set the output format to WebP.
  • Click Convert and download your WebP file.

Is it private?

Image conversions run entirely in your browser — your file is never uploaded to a server.
