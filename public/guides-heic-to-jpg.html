How to Convert HEIC to JPG

A quick, private way to make iPhone photos work everywhere.

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the format iPhones use by default. It saves space, but many apps, websites, and Windows programs still cannot open it. Converting HEIC to JPG fixes that instantly while keeping great image quality.

Why convert HEIC to JPG?

  • Compatibility: JPG opens on virtually every device and app.
  • Sharing: upload to sites and forms that reject HEIC.
  • Editing: most editors handle JPG without plugins.

Steps

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop your .heic file (or click browse).
  • Set the output format to JPG and adjust quality if you like.
  • Click Convert and download your JPG.

Is it private?

Yes. With FileMorph, image conversions run entirely in your browser — your photo is never uploaded to a server. That makes it ideal for personal pictures you would rather not send to the cloud.

Tips

Keep quality at 90%+ for photos you want to print. Lower it toward 70% for smaller files when sharing online.
