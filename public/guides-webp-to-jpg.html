How to Convert WebP to JPG

Turn web-downloaded WebP images into universally supported JPGs.

You saved an image from a website and it came down as a .webp file that your editor, Office, or an upload form won't accept. Converting WebP to JPG gives you the most universally compatible image format there is — perfect for sharing, uploading and printing.

WebP vs JPG

WebP is a modern format that makes web images smaller, but support outside the browser is still patchy. JPG is older but works literally everywhere. Both are lossy, so converting WebP to JPG keeps similar visual quality while giving you a file that any app or service will open.

Why convert WebP to JPG?

  • Universal support: JPG opens on every device, app and website.
  • Uploads & forms: portals that reject WebP accept JPG.
  • Printing & sharing: photo services and messaging apps prefer JPG.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop in your .webp file.
  • Set Convert to → JPG, and adjust quality if you like.
  • Click Convert and download your JPG.

One thing to know: transparency

If your WebP has a transparent background, JPG can't keep it — those areas become white. If you need transparency, convert to PNG instead. For ordinary photos, JPG is the smaller, friendlier choice.

Is it private?

Yes — it converts in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Will quality drop? Both are lossy; at high quality the JPG looks the same to the eye.

What about a transparent WebP? Use PNG to keep transparency — JPG fills it white.

Why was my download a WebP? The site served it that way for speed; converting makes it portable.
