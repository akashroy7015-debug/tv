How to Convert WebP to PNG

Get a widely supported image with lossless quality.

WebP is a modern format that keeps files small, but some older apps and tools still expect PNG. Converting WebP to PNG gives you a lossless image that works almost everywhere and supports transparency.

Why PNG?

  • Transparency is preserved.
  • Lossless quality — no compression artifacts.
  • Universal support across editors and platforms.

Steps

  • Open the converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop your .webp file.
  • Pick PNG as the output.
  • Click Convert and download.

Private by design

Like all image conversions on FileMorph, this happens locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Try it now: open the FileMorph converter, pick the category, drop your file, choose the output format, and click Convert.
