How to Convert PNG to JPG

Shrink large PNG screenshots and images into compact JPGs.

PNG is great for graphics and transparency, but it produces large files — especially for screenshots and photos. When you need a smaller image to email, upload, or attach, converting PNG to JPG can cut the file size dramatically while still looking great on screen.

Why convert PNG to JPG?

  • Smaller files: JPG compresses photos and screenshots far more than PNG.
  • Easier uploads: fits within size limits on forms and email.
  • Universal: JPG opens everywhere, just like PNG.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop in your .png file.
  • Set Convert to → JPG, and use the quality slider to balance size vs detail.
  • Click Convert and download your JPG.

One thing to know: transparency

JPG does not support transparency. If your PNG has a transparent background, those areas become white in the JPG. That's perfectly fine for photos and screenshots, but if you need to keep a transparent background (for a logo or overlay), stay with PNG or use WebP instead. See PNG vs JPG to decide.

Choosing quality

For sharing online, 70–85% quality gives a much smaller file that still looks sharp. Push to 90%+ for photos you'll print. There's rarely a need to go below 60%.

Is it private?

Yes — conversion happens in your browser, so your image is never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my transparent background turn white? JPG can't store transparency, so it's filled with white. Use PNG if you need it transparent.

How much smaller will it be? Often dramatically — screenshots and photos can shrink to a fraction of the PNG size.

Will it lose quality? JPG is lossy, but at 80%+ the difference is hard to see.
