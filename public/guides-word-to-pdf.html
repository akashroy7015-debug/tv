How to Convert Word (DOCX) to PDF

Lock your document into a polished file that looks the same everywhere.

You finished a document in Word, but when you send the DOCX the fonts shift, the layout breaks, or the other person can't open it. Converting Word to PDF solves all of that: a PDF looks identical on every device, can't be accidentally edited, and is the expected format for résumés, invoices, contracts and applications.

Why convert Word to PDF?

  • Consistent layout: fonts, spacing and page breaks stay exactly as you designed them.
  • Universal: anyone can open a PDF without Word installed.
  • Professional: the standard format for anything official.
  • Tamper-resistant: recipients see your final version, not an editable draft.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Document tab.
  • Drop in your .docx file.
  • Set Convert to → PDF.
  • Click Convert and download your PDF.

Tips for a clean PDF

Before converting, make sure your document looks right in Word — check page breaks, image placement and that any special fonts are common ones (very unusual fonts can be substituted). Conversion preserves your layout faithfully, so a tidy DOCX produces a tidy PDF. If your document has a cover page or headers/footers, they carry over too.

How FileMorph handles it

DOCX-to-PDF runs on FileMorph's secure conversion service using a full office engine, so complex formatting, tables and images are rendered accurately. Your file is used only for the conversion and isn't kept afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Will my formatting stay intact? Yes — layout, fonts (where standard), images and tables are preserved.

Can I edit the PDF later? PDFs aren't meant for heavy editing — keep your DOCX as the master copy, or convert PDF back to Word if needed.

Does it work with .doc as well as .docx? Modern .docx works best; older .doc files are also supported.
