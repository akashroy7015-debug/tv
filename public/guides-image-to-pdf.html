How to Convert an Image to PDF

Great for receipts, IDs, forms, and portfolios.

Need to email a photo as a document, or combine a scan into a paperwork bundle? Converting an image to PDF makes it look professional and ensures it prints at a predictable size.

When a PDF is better than an image

  • Forms & uploads that only accept PDF files.
  • Printing at a consistent page size.
  • Sharing documents that open the same on every device.

Steps

  • Open the converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop your PNG or JPG.
  • Choose PDF as the output format.
  • Click Convert and download your PDF.

How FileMorph builds the PDF

FileMorph creates a real, single-page PDF sized to your image — right inside your browser, with no upload. The result opens in any PDF reader and is ready to print or attach.
