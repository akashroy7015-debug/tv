How to Convert PDF to Word (DOCX)

Turn a fixed PDF back into an editable Word document.

PDFs are designed to look the same everywhere — which is great for sharing, but frustrating when you need to edit one. Converting a PDF to Word (DOCX) gives you back editable text you can change, copy, and reformat in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice. This guide explains how it works and what to expect.

Why convert PDF to Word?

  • Editing: change wording, fix typos, or update figures without recreating the document.
  • Reusing content: pull paragraphs, tables or lists into a new document.
  • Collaboration: DOCX is easy to track-change and comment on.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Document tab.
  • Drop in your .pdf file.
  • Set Convert to → DOCX.
  • Click Convert and download your Word document.

What to expect from the result

How cleanly a PDF converts depends on how it was made. A PDF that was exported from a word processor (real, selectable text) converts very well — paragraphs, headings and most formatting carry over. A PDF that is actually a scanned image of a page has no text layer, so the result may need more cleanup. Complex multi-column layouts, heavy graphics, and intricate tables are the hardest to reproduce perfectly, so always review the DOCX and tidy up spacing or tables as needed.

Tips for the best conversion

  • Start from a text-based PDF (try selecting text in it — if you can, you're good).
  • For scans, expect to do some manual formatting afterwards.
  • Keep the original PDF — conversion is one-way and you may want to redo it.

How FileMorph handles it

DOCX conversion is processed on FileMorph's secure conversion service, which uses a full document engine to rebuild the file. Your document is used only to perform the conversion and is not retained afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Will the formatting be perfect? For text-based PDFs it's usually very close; complex layouts may need minor cleanup.

Can it convert a scanned PDF? It will produce a document, but scanned pages without a text layer won't become fully editable text.

Is my file safe? Yes — it's processed only to convert and is not stored.
