How to Convert JPG to PNG

Get a lossless, edit-friendly image with transparency support.

JPG is great for small photos, but it's lossy — every save throws away a little detail, and it can't store transparency. Converting JPG to PNG gives you a lossless copy that's perfect for editing, adding a transparent background, or keeping text and edges crisp. Here's when to do it and how.

Why convert JPG to PNG?

  • Lossless quality: PNG won't add more compression artefacts as you edit and re-save.
  • Transparency: PNG supports a transparent background (JPG fills it with white).
  • Crisp edges & text: ideal for screenshots, logos and graphics.
  • Editing workflows: a clean base to work from in any editor.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Image tab.
  • Drop in your .jpg file.
  • Set Convert to → PNG.
  • Click Convert and download.

Important: PNG won't "add back" lost quality

A JPG has already discarded some detail. Converting to PNG preserves what's left perfectly, but it can't recover detail the JPG already removed. So the result looks identical to your JPG — just now in a lossless container you can edit safely. Expect the PNG to be larger, because lossless files store more data. If you only need a smaller photo, stay with JPG; see our PNG vs JPG guide.

Is it private?

Yes — the conversion happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Will the JPG look better as a PNG? It won't gain detail, but it won't lose any more — useful before editing.

Why is the PNG bigger? PNG is lossless, so it stores more data than the compressed JPG.

Does it add transparency? It supports transparency, but a JPG has none to begin with — you'd add it in an editor afterward.

Try it now: open the FileMorph converter, choose Image, drop your JPG, pick PNG, and click Convert.
