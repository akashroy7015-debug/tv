How to Convert MKV to MP4

Make MKV videos play on phones, TVs, editors and the web.

MKV (Matroska) is a flexible video container popular for high-quality downloads and rips — but it's not supported everywhere. Phones, smart TVs, browsers and many editors prefer MP4. Converting MKV to MP4 makes your video play and upload anywhere, usually without re-encoding the video itself.

MKV vs MP4

Both are "containers" that hold video + audio streams. MKV can wrap almost any codec and supports multiple audio/subtitle tracks, which is great for archiving but bad for compatibility. MP4 is the universal standard. Because they often hold the same H.264/HEVC video, converting is usually fast and keeps quality essentially identical.

Why convert MKV to MP4?

  • Plays everywhere: phones, TVs, browsers and consoles all support MP4.
  • Uploads: most platforms want MP4.
  • Editing: nearly every editor imports MP4 cleanly.

Step by step

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Video tab.
  • Drop in your .mkv file.
  • Set Convert to → MP4.
  • Click Convert. Larger videos take longer — a progress bar shows the work.
  • Download your MP4.

Notes on quality & tracks

Quality is typically preserved since the same video stream is reused. Note that MP4 handles fewer subtitle/audio tracks than MKV, so a movie with many embedded tracks will keep the main ones. Very large or 4K files take more time and processing — give it a moment.

Frequently asked questions

Will it lose quality? Usually not — the underlying video is kept intact in most cases.

Why is MKV not playing on my phone/TV? Many devices simply don't support the MKV container; MP4 fixes that.

What about subtitles? The primary track is kept; extra embedded tracks may be dropped.
