How to Convert MKV to MP4

Free, fast, and private — right in your browser.

MKV is a flexible open video container. MP4 is the most widely supported video format. Converting MKV to MP4 gives you a file that's easier to use, share, or open where MKV isn't supported.

Why convert MKV to MP4?

  • Plays on phones, TVs and every browser
  • Easy to upload and share
  • Great quality at a small size

Steps

  • Open the FileMorph converter and choose the Video tab.
  • Drop your .mkv file (or click browse).
  • Set the output format to MP4.
  • Click Convert and download your MP4 file.

Is it private?

Video converts right in your browser on your own device — nothing is uploaded. The first conversion loads the engine once; larger videos take longer.

Convert MKV to MP4 now: open the FileMorph converter, pick the Video tab, drop your file, and click Convert.
