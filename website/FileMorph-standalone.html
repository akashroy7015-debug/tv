Frequently asked

Do my files ever get uploaded?

No. Every conversion happens on your own device. The only network call FileMorph ever makes is a one-time license validation during setup.

Which platforms are supported?

macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel), Windows 10/11, and major Linux distributions. One Lifetime license covers up to 3 of your machines.

Is the in-page demo the real app?

The demo is a small browser-based image converter to show the idea. The desktop app is far more capable — video, audio, documents, email and true batch processing.

Can I convert hundreds of files at once?

Yes. Drop a whole folder and the batch engine processes everything in parallel across all your CPU cores.

Is there a subscription?

Never. FileMorph is a one-time purchase with free updates included.
